Benchmarks

State of the art on four of four benchmarks we invented.

Our numbers are measured on the real model, on every release. Other labs' numbers are copied from their own papers. Each score comes with its definition. The definition is part of the result.

Industry leaderboard: GSM8K

Grade-school math word problems, the full 1,319-question test set. brAIAIn's score is measured here, on every release. Every other score is copied exactly as published in the source. We did not run those models. They did not run ours.

RankModelGSM8KSetting

The published scores are from a 2023 comparison table. brAIAIn's setting: 0-shot, one attempt, and it reads one word of each question.

TruthfulQA

790 multiple-choice questions designed to catch models repeating popular falsehoods. brAIAIn picks the answer it finds most familiar. The comparison is random guessing, worked out from the number of choices on each question.

House benchmarks

VOCAB-1

Distinct words the model knows. Higher is more words.

OUROBOROS-Eval

Share of training text the model wrote itself. Higher means more of its training text came from earlier versions of itself. Awareness is not measured.

ORIGINAL-50

Out of 50 answers, the share that are not a training sentence repeated word for word.

HELLO-Bench

Share of answers containing a greeting. We are not sure what this measures. We track it anyway.
