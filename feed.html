What happens

  1. You pay and type your sentence, up to 255 characters, on the checkout page. You can add a name to be credited, or stay anonymous.
  2. Usually within half an hour, a new version of brAIAIn is trained with your sentence in it.
  3. It gets a model card, release notes, a research paper and a status incident, written in the house style.
  4. Your sentence becomes part of every future model. Each new version is trained on all previous sentences, so it never fully goes away.

What gets your sentence left out

Links, email addresses, phone numbers, account handles, and words our safety team would not like. If your sentence is left out, you get a refund. If a name you give is left out, your sentence is still used and credited to Anonymous.

What you are buying

One sentence in the training data of a small language model, and the release that goes with it. That is all. It is not an investment, an advertisement or a share in anything. Read the terms.
