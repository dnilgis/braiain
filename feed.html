What happens

  1. You pay and type your sentence, up to 255 characters, on the checkout page. You can add a name to be credited, or stay anonymous. Your sentence and credit are public, so leave out anything private.
  2. A person reviews it, usually within a day. Releases go out in the order they are approved.
  3. A new version of brAIAIn is trained with your sentence in it, with a model card, release notes, a research paper and a status incident.
  4. Your sentence stays in the training data of every later version. That guarantees it is in the training data, not that it appears in any particular answer.

What gets a sentence left out

Links, contact details, account handles, advertisements, other people's private information, and anything hateful or sexual. If screening or review leaves your sentence out, you get a full refund. A name or codename that does not pass is dropped, and the sentence is still used.

Refunds

Full refund if your sentence is not approved, if no release appears within two days, or if something goes wrong on our side. You can also ask for a refund at any time before your sentence is approved. After it is published, it is not refundable. Email hello@braiain.com.

What you are buying

A sentence in the training data of a small language model, and the release that goes with it. It is not an investment, an advertisement or a share in anything. Read the terms.
