How many models are on Hugging Face?

As of October 16, 2026, Hugging Face hosts 3,157,435 public model repositories — up from 31,709 in March 2022, a 99.6x increase. Across twelve archived snapshots the count has doubled every 8.1 months on average (log-linear fit, R² = 0.957) — and the pace has been slowing: the most recent interval, March to October 2026 (+18%), implies a ~32-month doubling.

Method & caveats

Each point is the numTotalItems counter machine-read from an Internet Archive capture of huggingface.co/models, dated by the capture's Memento-Datetime header — the page's own number, archived, not estimated. The count is model repositories: forks, finetunes and duplicates all count, so this measures publishing activity, not distinct capabilities. The doubling time is a least-squares fit on log2(count) over 12 points — a 2022–2026 average, not the current pace (recent intervals are slower), and not a forecast.

Questions

Does this count distinct models?
No — repositories. A base model and its thousands of finetunes each count once. It is a measure of open-model publishing activity.
What is the growth rate?
Doubling every 8.1 months over Mar 2022–Oct 2026 (log-linear fit, R²=0.957, n=12). At the pace measured between the last two snapshots, roughly one new repository lands every 41 seconds.
Is the live number different?
The live counter read 3,169,809 on Oct 20, 2026 — slightly above the Oct 16 archived point, as expected.
