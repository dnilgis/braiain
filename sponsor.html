BRAIAIN Sponsorship Last updated: 2026-10-29

One sponsor. Here is exactly how it works.

BRAIAIN is a sourced reference on the AI buildout, built to be cited. It carries no ad network and never will. It runs on a single exclusive sponsorship, and this page says plainly what that is — in the same spirit as the rest of the site: no claims we can't back.

What the sponsor gets

  • Exclusivity — one sponsor, no competing logos, no ad clutter anywhere on the site.
  • A clearly labeled placement on the board and the reference pages (capex, GPU prices, PJM, data centers) — never beside the editorial Reckoning section.
  • A tagged link and, once analytics exist, a monthly report of real impressions and clicks.
  • Association with the citability assets: published methodology, per-figure sources, downloadable data, a permanent archive, and a public corrections log.

What we can prove today — honestly

The board launched October 20, 2026. We do not yet publish audience numbers because we do not yet measure them; analytics and Search Console are being set up. We will never pitch invented traffic. When numbers exist, sponsors see the real ones — the same discipline the site applies to every other figure.

What is not for sale

  • Editorial influence. No input on what the board measures, says, or corrects.
  • The data. Downloads stay free (CC BY 4.0); embeds stay free.
  • Softer numbers. A sponsor uncomfortable with a sourced figure is welcome to stop sponsoring.

Structure

First engagement is a measured 30-day pilot: labeled placement, tagged link, and a written results report with whatever can honestly be measured by then. Pricing is a conversation sized to the evidence available — it will be small until the audience is proven, and it will never be justified with numbers we can't show.
