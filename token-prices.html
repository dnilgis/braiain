What do LLM tokens cost?

As of October 2026, flagship list prices are $50 per million output tokens (OpenAI GPT-6 Astra; Anthropic Claude Fable 5.1), with the strong mid-tier at $10 (GPT-6.1 Sol, Claude Sonnet 5.5, Gemini 4 Argon). Flagship prices have not collapsed — OpenAI's rose from GPT-4 Turbo's $30 in 2023 to $50, while Anthropic's eased from Claude 3 Opus's $75 to $50 — but the strong mid tier fell to $10 (Gemini's rises to $20 on Dec 1, 2026): a two-tier market.

Method & caveats

Providers' own published list prices per million tokens, with historical points from dated announcements. List prices ignore volume discounts, caching and batch pricing, and models across generations are not like-for-like in capability — the comparison describes the shape of the market (a persistent frontier premium above a cheapening mid-tier), not price per unit of intelligence. Pricing pages are undated; fetch dates recorded.

Questions

Is the price of AI falling?
Not at the frontier: OpenAI's flagship rose from $30 (2023) to $50, and Anthropic's eased from $75 (2024) to $50. Below the frontier, yes: strong mid-tier models list at $10, though Gemini's goes to $20 on Dec 1, 2026. A two-tier market, not a collapse.
Are these the prices companies actually pay?
These are list prices. Enterprise discounts, caching and batch APIs reduce real costs; those prices are not public.
