What you buy

For the listed price, in US dollars, one sentence you write is reviewed and, if approved, added to the public training data of brAIAIn, and a new model version is released with it. The $15 tier adds a printable certificate and a name in the paper's acknowledgments. The $50 tier adds a codename for that version, which is reviewed like the sentence. You get no ownership, share, revenue, or other rights in the site or the model.

Review, removal and refunds

Every sentence is screened automatically and reviewed by a person before it is published. You get a full refund if your sentence is not approved, if no release appears within two days of payment, or if something goes wrong on our side. You can also ask for a refund by email at any time before your sentence is approved. Refunds go back to the original card; your bank may take several business days to show them. After publication, purchases are not refundable.
