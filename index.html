Methodology & sources Changelog

Every figure on this board comes from the public source named beside it, as of the date shown. Ticking figures are arithmetic on those sources — a filed annual pace divided to the second, or an interpolation between two dated statements — never a live measurement. The Reckoning is an editorial estimate with its full math published; it is not a prediction market, a poll of experts, or anyone's forecast but ours.
