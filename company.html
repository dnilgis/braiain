About

BRAIAIN Laboratories was founded to answer one question: would anyone pay for this. Research continues.

Unlike other labs, we do not train on the open internet, on books, or on anything we found lying around. We train only on sentences people give us, for money, on purpose. We believe this makes brAIAIn the most ethically sourced language model in the world, and also the smallest.

A note on how this board was assembled

Directors were selected through a rigorous search lasting one afternoon. Candidates were evaluated on availability, silence in meetings, and willingness to be photographed. Every director serves without compensation, and most without awareness.

Board meetings are held whenever all eight members are in the same room, which is always. Minutes are not kept, because nothing has ever been said. All decisions to date have been unanimous.
