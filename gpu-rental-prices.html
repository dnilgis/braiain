What does it cost to rent an H100?

As of the week of October 12, 2026, the median on-demand price across providers is $3.50 per GPU-hour for an H100 and $4.88 for an H200 — up from $2.99 and $3.50 a year earlier: +17% and +39% in twelve months, over a period in which chip performance per dollar has been improving ~49% a year (Epoch AI). Lambda's own list price for an H100 SXM is $3.99/GPU-hour.

Method & caveats

Sampled weekly on-demand provider medians (five sampled weeks across the year) from GetDeploying's GPU price dataset (CC BY 4.0, credit GetDeploying); the series starts Oct 13, 2025. A provider median can move when the provider mix changes, and on-demand is the most expensive way to buy compute — reserved and contract prices are lower and not covered. The Epoch performance-per-dollar trend is a multi-year hardware trend, not a measurement over this exact window; the contrast between the two is the point, with that caveat attached.

Questions

Why are rental prices rising if chips keep improving?
Chip performance per dollar improves ~49%/yr (Epoch), yet the median H100 rent rose 17% in a year — a sign demand for compute is outrunning the hardware improvement, at least on the on-demand market for this SKU.
Is $3.50 the market price of compute?
No — it is the median on-demand price for one chip model across listed providers. Reserved capacity and private contracts trade lower and are not public.
