Privacy.

What we collect is short. So is this page.

This site is operated from Wisconsin, USA. Contact: hello@braiain.com.

The chat

The model runs in your browser. What you type into the chat is not sent to us or anyone else, and is not stored.

If you buy a sentence

Payment is handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your card details, email and billing information under its own privacy policy; we see your email, the amount, and the sentence, credit name and codename you enter. We keep payment records as long as tax law requires. Your sentence, credit name and codename are published on this site and in its public source code on GitHub once approved. Rejected or pending sentences are never published.

If you join the waitlist

Your email address is collected by Formspree, a form service, and used only to send one message when paid training opens. We delete the list after that message is sent. Ask us to delete yours sooner at any time.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics, which sets cookies, to count visits and see which pages are read. Google processes this data under its own terms. You can block it with your browser's privacy settings or an extension; the site works without it.

Your choices

Email hello@braiain.com to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, or to remove your published sentence or name. Removal takes it off the live site and out of every retrained model. It cannot erase copies that already exist elsewhere, such as the public history of the source files or copies made by others.

Children

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
