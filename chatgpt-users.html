How many people use ChatGPT?

OpenAI's most recent statement puts ChatGPT at 1.2 billion weekly users (Sep 29, 2026, repeated Oct 7). The company said 900 million on Feb 27, 2026 — growth of about 1.4 million users a day between the two statements. The timeline below lists every official figure back to 100 million weekly users in November 2023, with weaker secondhand points labeled.

Method & caveats

Only figures OpenAI itself stated (keynotes, blog posts) or direct reports of such statements are included; each point carries a provenance label and weaker points are marked. The series is a set of dated statements, not a measured curve — interpolation between points is labeled wherever used. All figures are weekly users as OpenAI stated them.

Questions

How fast is ChatGPT growing?
Between OpenAI's Feb 27 and Sep 29, 2026 statements: 300 million additional weekly users in 214 days — about 1.4 million a day, or 16 every second, measured between those two statements.
When did it pass 1 billion?
Interpolating between the two 2026 statements puts the billion crossing around May 9, 2026. OpenAI did not announce the crossing itself.
Are these monthly or weekly users?
Weekly, in every figure shown, as OpenAI stated them.
