Every hyperscaler nuclear deal, listed

Big-tech buyers have announced 7,754 MW of nuclear capacity across six named deals between September 2024 and January 2026: Meta leads with 4,499 MW (Vistra, Oklo, TerraPower), Amazon 1,920 MW (Talen/Susquehanna), Microsoft 835 MW (Constellation's Three Mile Island Unit 1 restart, targeted 2027), Google 500 MW (Kairos, with TVA). Of the total, 2,390 MW is small or advanced reactors (Oklo, TerraPower, Kairos) — the newest reactor designs' first anchor customers are AI companies. No new deal has been announced since January 2026.

Method & caveats

Announced capacity from SEC filings and company releases — not operating megawatts: the earliest delivery is the Crane restart targeted for 2027, and several deals run to 2030–2035. Deal MW types differ (PPAs on operating plants vs new construction). "None since January 2026" describes this log of named deals, not a claim about private negotiations.

Questions

How much of this is actually running?
Meta's Vistra deals cover 2,176 MW of already-operating plants under new 20-year PPAs. Everything else is restarts or new construction — the first, Three Mile Island Unit 1 for Microsoft, is targeted for 2027.
Is 7,754 MW a lot?
For scale: PJM's last capacity auction cleared 6,831 MW short of its reliability requirement — roughly the size of everything hyperscalers announced across these 16 months of deals. Announced is not built, and the deals are not all in PJM.
