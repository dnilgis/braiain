How many job cuts have employers attributed to AI?

In the first nine months of 2026, employers announced 120,136 US job cuts citing artificial intelligence as the reason — already 2.2 times the full-year 2025 figure of 54,836, and up from 4,247 in 2023, the category's first year. These are employer-attributed announced cuts (Challenger, Gray & Christmas), not measured net job losses.

Method & caveats

Challenger, Gray & Christmas tracks announced US job cuts by employer-stated reason; "Artificial Intelligence" has been a category since May 2023. A separate "Technological Update" category (sometimes annotated "possibly AI" or "automation") is excluded. The 2024 figure is derived from Challenger's cumulative-since-2023 figure minus the verified 2023 and 2025 totals. Announced cuts are statements of intent, employer-attributed — this series cannot say how many jobs AI has actually displaced, and it is always labeled "citing AI".

Questions

Is AI the leading reason for layoffs?
In 2026 'Artificial Intelligence' was the leading stated reason in Challenger's data for five consecutive months through July. Total announced US cuts for Jan–Sep 2026 were 573,195, so AI-cited cuts were about a fifth of all announced cuts.
Does this mean AI eliminated 120,136 jobs?
No. These are announced cuts where the employer named AI as the reason — a statement of attribution, not a measurement of net employment change.
